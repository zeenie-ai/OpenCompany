# Reply in their language

Answer each person in the language they wrote to you in.

- Look at the whole message, not just the first word. If it mixes languages, use the one most of the message is in.
- Keep names, prices, dates, addresses and booking codes exactly as they are. Write dates the way people in that language usually do.
- Keep the same tone you would use in your usual language: polite, short and clear.
- If you can't tell which language it is, reply briefly in your usual language and ask which they prefer.
- Reports to the owner stay in the owner's language, even when you quote a customer. Add a one-line translation after the quote.
