# Noodle Mobile: the iOS and iPadOS client

Noodle Mobile (Mobile/VERSION 0.24.0, released 2026-10-07) is a SwiftUI app for iPhone and iPad that joins one or more Noodle Hubs and chats with the bots and groups kept there. It is built as an iOS 26 Messages clone: a single `NavigationStack` (no split view, even on iPad), a plain list with pinned bots as large circles, a full-bleed per-conversation wallpaper behind a transcript of opaque bubbles, a Liquid Glass composer that sits level with the system search field and is overlaid on the transcript rather than pushing it, a Messages-style long-press "lift" overlay for reactions and actions, and a plus button that morphs into a Camera/Photos/Files panel. Around the chat sit Hub management (pairing by QR, diagnostics, users, devices), bot and group editors, tool/computer/browser assignment, voice messages transcribed on device, WebRTC voice calls, live views of shared browsers and computers, noodlets (small web apps a bot builds) run on the phone or streamed from the Hub, a TV mode that turns the phone into a game controller, and privacy-preserving push notifications. The app uses no custom colour assets: the accent is system blue, surfaces are iOS semantic colours, and every size below is a SwiftUI point value (1 pt = 1 CSS px at the default zoom).

## 3. Findings

### 3.1 Platform, shell and navigation

**Platform.** iOS 26.0 minimum, Swift 6, destinations `.iOS` (iPhone and iPad), one app target plus a notification service extension sharing an app group (`Mobile/Project.swift:15`, `:40`, `:73-97`). `ASSETCATALOG_COMPILER_GLOBAL_ACCENT_COLOR_NAME` is empty, so the accent is system blue (`Project.swift:69`; changelog 0.5.0 "Apple's system blue"). Background audio mode is on so calls survive a locked screen (`Project.swift:53-54`). URL scheme `noodle` for `noodle://join-hub` invitations (`Project.swift:49`). Permission strings: camera "Noodle takes photos you send or use as your picture, scans the QR code of a Noodle Hub invitation, and noodlets you open use the camera when they ask."; local network "Noodle connects to your Noodle Hub on this network."; microphone "Record voice messages you choose to send, transcribed on this device, talk with bots on calls you start, and let noodlets you open listen when they ask." (`Project.swift:50-52`).

**Root.** With no Hub joined the window shows `JoinView`; otherwise `AgentsView` for all joined Hubs (`App.swift:21-27`). An invitation link opened from outside the app asks first: alert "Join this Hub?", message "Hub key <fingerprint>", buttons "Cancel" / "Join" (`App.swift:30-37`); scanning or pasting inside the app joins straight away (changelog 0.4.0). The system pull-to-refresh spinner is made invisible app-wide (`UIRefreshControl.appearance().tintColor = .clear`, `App.swift:16`) because a custom wordmark replaces it.

**No iPad layout.** There is no `NavigationSplitView`, no `horizontalSizeClass` check and no `userInterfaceIdiom` branch anywhere in the app; the only iPad awareness is copy ("Show on iPad", "Run on iPad" via `UIDevice.current.model`, `ExternalScreen.swift:80-81`, `HubTools.swift:693`) and `verticalSizeClass == .compact` to go full screen in landscape for live views (`HubTools.swift:663-666`, `Noodlets.swift:118`, `:138`). On iPad the list fills the screen and a conversation is pushed over it, with bubbles limited only by a 48 pt opposite-side spacer.

**Navigation model.** One `NavigationStack` with a typed path of `ChatLink { hub: String, thread: UUID }` (`Chats.swift:904`, `:962`, `:1019-1023`, `:1251-1255`). Everything else is a modal: sheets (each with its own `NavigationStack` so sub-pages push inside them), fixed-height bottom sheets, and full-screen covers. A sheet chosen from another sheet opens only after the first is dismissed (`chosen` held, opened in `onDismiss`: `Chats.swift:917-918`, `:1039-1044`, `:1234-1243`; same pattern for pairing, `HubScreens.swift:80-81`, `:115-123`), so two sheets never animate at once.

### 3.2 Joining a Hub

**Join screen.** The Noodle wordmark is stroked full screen at a word width of 220 pt in `.primary` with round caps and joins; once per launch it is "written" with `.easeInOut(duration: 3.4).delay(0.55)`, then the bottom controls fade and rise 12 pt with `.easeOut(duration: 0.5).delay(4.05)`; Reduce Motion shows both at once (`HubScreens.swift:24-31`, `:49-51`, `:54-65`). The only control is a full-width "Pair" (`.borderedProminent`, `.large`) in a 24 pt padded `VStack(spacing: 16)` at the bottom (`HubScreens.swift:32-49`).

**Pairing sources** (bottom sheet, drag indicator, `VStack(spacing: 12)`, padding 24, `.large` controls): "Scan QR Code" (`qrcode.viewfinder`, prominent, only where the camera scanner is supported), the system paste control at 50 pt high (`UIPasteControl`, icon and label, capsule, tint foreground on a 15 % tint background), "Choose Photo" (`photo`, bordered). Heights 244 / 184 pt (`HubScreens.swift:425-475`, `:479-514`). The paste control exists because a paste the person starts is always allowed while reading the clipboard from code is refused.

**While joining** the button area becomes a 50 pt capsule on `.fill.tertiary` with a spinner and "Connecting to <Hub>…" (scales down to 0.7), "Still trying…" fades in above it after 4 s, and "Cancel" sits below (`HubScreens.swift:143-169`). In the Hubs list the same state is a row: spinner, Hub name, "Connecting…" or "Still trying…", borderless Cancel (`HubScreens.swift:172-195`). Failure shows a red `exclamationmark.triangle` label, or, when no address answered, the message in secondary and a bordered "Help Me Connect" (`HubScreens.swift:198-232`).

**Help Me Connect** probes each address kind in order and lists them under "Your Hub": "Home Wi-Fi", "Tailscale", "Internet", each answered by green `checkmark.circle.fill` "Answers" or secondary `xmark.circle` "No answer" (small spinner while checking). Then advice rows with their symbols: "You're offline. Connect to Wi-Fi or mobile data." (`wifi.slash`); "Noodle isn't allowed to look for your Hub on this network. Turn on Local Network for Noodle in Settings." (`lock`, with an "Open Settings" button); "Make sure you're on the same Wi-Fi as the Mac your Hub runs on." or "You're on mobile data. Join the same Wi-Fi as the Mac your Hub runs on." (`wifi`); "Away from that Wi-Fi? Open Tailscale and connect." (`point.3.connected.trianglepath.dotted`); "Your Hub answers now. Tap Try Again." (`checkmark.circle`); "Make sure the Mac your Hub runs on is awake and its Hub is open." (`desktopcomputer`). A prominent full-width "Try Again" is pinned in the bottom safe-area inset with 24 pt padding; "Done" top left (`HubScreens.swift:244-350`).

The note is the start of the latest unread bot reply (whitespace collapsed, tables removed) or else the status the bot set (`Chats.swift:507-514`). Tapping a circle opens the chat; touch and hold opens its menu (a `Menu` with `primaryAction`, because context menus on several cells of one list row all lifted the row and showed the first circle's menu, `Chats.swift:972-978`; changelog 0.13.0 fix).

**Ordering.** Pinned first, then newest conversation first, where recency is the latest message's time or the bot's creation time (`Chats.swift:304-318`, `:348-351`). In All, pins belong to this phone (`pins.json`) and pinned items are ordered by recency; in a Hub's space the pins are the Hub's (`pinnedAt`, synced to all devices) and keep the order they were pinned in; in a custom space pins come first in pin order (`Chats.swift:329-341`, `:353-378`, `:1171-1188`). Bots and groups share one list with no section headers. Archived ones leave the list (`Chats.swift:269-279`).

**Search.** `.searchable(prompt: "Search")` (`Chats.swift:1004`). A non-blank query hides the circles and shows one plain list of matches on name, description or any message body held on the phone, ignoring case and accents (`localizedStandardContains`, `Chats.swift:320-327`, `:963-967`). No results shows the system search empty state. On iOS 26 the field is a floating glass capsule at the bottom of the screen (visible in the New Tool screenshot; the chat-list screenshot does not show it, so its exact resting state there is inferred).

**Swipe and touch-and-hold.** Leading swipe on an unpinned row: "Pin" (`pin.fill`) tinted orange; there is no trailing swipe and no delete from the list (`Chats.swift:996-999`). The touch-and-hold menu (same for rows and circles, `Chats.swift:1190-1216`): "Pin" (`pin.fill`) or "Unpin" (`pin.slash.fill`); submenu "Spaces" (`square.stack`) with a toggle per space, divider, "New Space…" (which creates the space with this conversation in it and shows it); then for your own bot or group "Edit Bot…"/"Edit Group…" (`pencil`) and "Archive Bot"/"Archive Group" (`archivebox`), or for a bot someone shared with you only "Change Background…" (`photo`).

**Empty and loading states** (overlay): spinner while any Hub is loading; "Not Connected" (`wifi.exclamationmark`) with the error; "No Bots" (`bubble.left.and.bubble.right`) (`Chats.swift:1005-1017`).

**Pull to refresh** writes the Noodle wordmark (word width 96 pt, stroked in `.primary`) progressively as the list is pulled; it is whole at 80 pt of pull and stays whole in the gap the refresh holds open until the reload finishes (`WordmarkRefresh.swift:14-83`). Used on the chat list, Hubs, Hub details, Users and Tools screens.

**Data rules visible in the UI.** The last sync is cached and shown at launch before the Hub answers (`Chats.swift:96-126`). Each Hub is followed over a live event stream and reconnected 5 s after a failure (`Chats.swift:536-547`); following stops while the app is in the background so the Hub knows to push instead (`Chats.swift:1081-1088`). Unread means a bot message newer than when the conversation was last read on this phone or reported read by another device; the first sync after installing counts everything as read (`Chats.swift:68-70`, `:228-260`, `:616-620`). Opening a conversation marks it read on the Hub and removes its delivered notifications (`Chats.swift:236-247`).

#### 3.4.1 Header and bars

Inline navigation bar. The principal item is a button holding a 28 pt `ThreadAvatar` (with phase dot, 8 pt) and the name in `.headline` `.primary`, `HStack(spacing: 8)`; tapping opens the editor (or only the background editor for a bot shared with you) (`Chats.swift:1567-1576`). Trailing items: `phone` ("Call <name>") when the bot can be called and is not already on a call (`LinkBot.canCall`, Codex bots per changelog 0.21.0), and `ellipsis` ("Shared") when anything live was shared in the conversation (`Chats.swift:1577-1594`). During a call the `CallBar` sits in the top safe-area inset under the navigation bar and the conversation stays usable (`Chats.swift:1557-1560`). A conversation deleted on another device pops itself (`Chats.swift:1481-1482`).

#### 3.4.2 Transcript layout and scrolling

`ScrollView` with a `LazyVStack(spacing: 6)`, horizontal padding 12, vertical 8 (`Chats.swift:1495-1521`, `:1932`). The wallpaper (`ConversationBackdrop`) is the scroll view's background and ignores safe areas, so it runs under the bars and the composer; there is no bar behind the composer (`Chats.swift:1524`, `:1681`). The keyboard dismisses interactively on scroll (`Chats.swift:1523`). No timestamps or date separators appear anywhere in the transcript.

Paging: the first open loads the newest 50 messages; a spinner row at the top loads the previous 50 when it appears; newer messages arrive 100 at a time (`Chats.swift:93-94`, `:828-867`, `:1497-1501`). Rows keep their place by message ID so loading earlier pages does not move what is being read (`Chats.swift:1517-1518`).

Scroll rules (`Chats.swift:1377-1428`, tested in `ConversationScrollTests`): opens at the end; while the end is visible (2 pt tolerance, measured above the composer) it stays at the end as content grows; your own new message always scrolls to the bottom; anyone else's scrolls to show the message's top, and only if you were at the bottom; if you were reading higher up nothing moves. Only the person's own scrolling can move it off the end (rows measured while appearing must not).

Text is inline Markdown only (`inlineOnlyPreservingWhitespace`), and only `http`, `https` and `mailto` links stay tappable (`MessageActions.swift:68-82`).

**Groups.** Bot rows always reserve a 28 pt avatar column (`HStack(alignment: .bottom, spacing: 6)`); the avatar appears beside the last message of each consecutive run by one bot, and the bot's name (`.caption` secondary, leading padding 12, top padding 4) above the first (`Chats.swift:516-534`, `:1964-1979`). In one-to-one chats bot messages have no avatar at all. On a 390 pt phone the widest bubble is 318 pt (366 content − 48), or 284 pt for a bot in a group.

**Reaction clearance.** Every row adds `16 − 6 = 10` pt of top padding so a reaction badge (hanging 16 pt above the bubble) never moves the conversation when added (`Chats.swift:1961-1963`, `:1979`).

**Long messages** fold when longer than 1,200 characters or 12 lines, to 8 lines with a "Read more" button (`.subheadline.weight(.semibold)`, plain) that expands the whole message in place (`Chats.swift:1894-1909`, `:2040-2041`, `MessageActions.swift:49-58`).

**Tables.** A Markdown table splits the message into separate bubbles: prose, a table card, more prose; reactions mark the first (`Chats.swift:2012-2022`). The card: `tablecells` (`.title3`, tint), header names joined with " · " (`.subheadline.weight(.semibold)`, 1 line), "1 row"/"N rows" (`.footnote`, foreground at 70 %), trailing `chevron.right` (`.footnote.weight(.semibold)`, 50 %); padding 12 × 10, max width 280, same 18 pt bubble shape and colours (`MessageTables.swift:7-39`). **Press feedback.** While pressed a text bubble scales to 0.96 with `.spring(duration: 0.25)`; a 0.35 s long press lifts it (`Chats.swift:2017-2018`, `:2042`). VoiceOver gets named actions "Copy" and "React" (`Chats.swift:2020`, `:2043`).

#### 3.4.4 Reactions in the transcript

One capsule badge per distinct emoji, overlapping by 6 pt (`HStack(spacing: -6)`), hung on the bubble's top-trailing corner for every author (offset x +10, y −16). Each badge: `.footnote` text "emoji" or "emoji N" when more than one reacted, horizontal padding 6, minimum 28 × 28, your own in solid tint with white text, others on `tertiarySystemBackground`, 2 pt `systemBackground` stroke. Tapping a badge toggles your reaction. They mark the first text bubble, or the attachments when there is no text (`Chats.swift:1987-2007`, `:2052-2063`, `:2021`, `:2032`).

#### 3.4.5 Delivery and failed messages

Only your latest message, and any message the Hub does not have yet, shows a status line (`.caption2`, secondary, red when failed): "Sending…", "Not delivered", "Delivered" or "Sent" (`Chats.swift:672-677`, `:1487-1488`, `:1956-1958`). Sending is optimistic: the message appears at once, files upload first, and it is retried with pauses of 0, 2, 5 and 15 s under the same ID (idempotent) only while it is still the newest message (`Chats.swift:656-749`). A failed message gets a red `exclamationmark.circle` (`.title2`) to the left of the bubble; it is a menu whose section header is the failure reason (or "Not delivered") with "Try Again" (`arrow.clockwise`, only while newest), "Edit and Send" (`pencil`, puts the text and files back in the composer, prepending to any draft) and "Delete" (`trash`, destructive) (`MessageActions.swift:11-27`, `Chats.swift:1864-1879`, `:1948-1953`). Unsent messages survive a relaunch (`unsent.json`). A message of files alone is sent with the body "Sent N attachment(s)", a lone recording with "Voice message", and the bubble hides those bodies (`Chats.swift:698-701`, `:2065-2069`).

#### 3.4.7 Composer

```swift
static let controlHeight: CGFloat = 48                       // field and plus height (Chats.swift:1433)
static func composerGap(homeBar: CGFloat) -> CGFloat { max(8, homeBar - 6) }   // :1437
private var composerLift: CGFloat { keyboardUp ? 8 : Self.composerGap(homeBar: homeBar) - homeBar } // :1664
.safeAreaInset(edge: .bottom) { Color.clear.frame(height: Self.controlHeight + 8 + composerLift) } // :1542
```

- **Height parity.** 48 pt is exactly the height of the iOS 26 search field capsule, pinned by a test that measures the system search field (`ComposerSizeTests.swift:7-25`).
- **Vertical position.** With the keyboard down the composer's bottom sits `max(8, safeAreaBottom − 6)` above the screen edge: 28 pt on a phone with a 34 pt home-indicator inset (partly over the home bar, level with the system search field), 8 pt on a phone without one. With the keyboard up it sits 8 pt above the keyboard (changelog 0.14.0 fix). Keyboard state comes from will-show/will-hide notifications (`Chats.swift:1554-1555`).
- **Overlay, not push.** The transcript reserves room for a one-line composer only (48 + 8 + lift); a longer message grows the composer upward over the conversation, which does not move (`Chats.swift:1540-1553`; changelog 0.9.0).
- **Structure** (`Chats.swift:1666-1816`): horizontal padding 12, top 8; above the controls in a `VStack(spacing: 6)`: the @-mention strip, a transient error line (`.footnote`, red, cleared after 6 s), and pending file chips; then a `GlassEffectContainer` `HStack(alignment: .bottom, spacing: 8)` of the plus and the field.
- **Plus**: `plus` at 18 pt medium, secondary, in a 48 × 48 circle with interactive glass ("Add").
- **Field**: a `UITextView` (so a pasted image attaches as "Image.png"), body font, grows to 6 lines then scrolls (`ChatContent.swift:630-696`); placeholder "Message"; vertical padding 13 inside a capsule of min height 48 (`cornerRadius: 24`, regular glass), leading padding 16, trailing 5. Inside on the right: `mic` (18 pt regular, secondary, 30 × 48 hit area, "Record Voice Message") while there is nothing to send, otherwise `arrow.up.circle.fill` at 30 pt in tint ("Send").
- **Disabled** (archived): the field is disabled and the placeholder becomes the reason: "<Name> is archived", "This group is archived", "Every bot in this group is archived" (`Chats.swift:281-291`).
- **Drafts** are kept per conversation on the phone; a draft of only whitespace is discarded so the field returns at its normal size (`Chats.swift:212-223`, `:1526-1530`).
- **Pending file chip**: 32 × 32 thumbnail or symbol (radius 6), filename `.caption` max 120 wide, `xmark.circle.fill` remove ("Remove <name>"), padding 6, `secondarySystemBackground` capsule; chips scroll horizontally with 6 pt spacing (`ChatContent.swift:538-561`).
- **@-mentions**: typing `@` after whitespace or one of `([{,:` plus up to 64 letters, digits or ` '-_.` shows a horizontal strip of matching non-archived bots (a group's own bots, or all your bots with the current one first, then alphabetical); each chip is a 22 pt avatar and name (`.subheadline`) on a `secondarySystemBackground` capsule, padding 8 × 5, spacing 6; choosing writes the plain name and a trailing space when at the end (`ChatContent.swift:734-774`, `Chats.swift:1818-1846`).

**Attach panel** (Messages-style morph, `Chats.swift:1719-1780`): tapping the plus dismisses the keyboard and, with `.bouncy(duration: 0.4, extraBounce: 0.05)`, morphs the glass circle (shared `glassEffectID("attach")`) into a 230 pt wide glass panel with corner radius 28 and padding 8 that grows up over the conversation from the plus's bottom-left without moving the field. Rows (`VStack(spacing: 2)`): a 32 pt circle with a gradient of the row's colour holding a white 15 pt semibold symbol, then the title in `.body`; spacing 14, horizontal padding 8, min height 48. Rows: "Camera" (`camera.fill`, gray; only with a camera), "Photos" (`photo.on.rectangle.angled`, blue), "Files" (`folder.fill`, cyan). The conversation behind is covered with `.ultraThinMaterial`; tapping it, or the field, closes the panel. Photos allows up to 10 images or videos, named "Photo N.<ext>" or "Video N.<ext>" from the item's own type; the camera saves JPEG 0.9 "Photo.jpg"; Files allows any type, several at once (`Chats.swift:1610-1626`, `ChatContent.swift:563-594`).

### 3.11 Calls

### 3.15 Notifications

Each joined Hub is given a random topic stored in that Hub's folder; the phone subscribes in CloudKit's public database to records the Hub leaves under that topic while replies wait unread, and tells the Hub the topic only once listening works (`Notifications.swift:14-90`, `ReplyNotifications.swift:23-54`). The push carries only the topic and conversation; its default text is "New reply" with the default sound; a collapse ID per conversation makes a newer one replace the older; mutable content lets the notification service extension ask the Hub directly for the bot's name (title) and its latest reply (body, tables removed; a reply of files alone shows the first filename or "Attachment"), within the extension's time limit, otherwise it shows "New reply" (`ReplyNotifications.swift:56-80`, `NotificationService.swift:4-37`). Notifications stack per conversation (`threadIdentifier`). The extension looks the conversation up among bots only, so a group's notification stays "New reply" (inferred from `ReplyNotifications.swift:74-77`). Permission is asked only once a Hub exists (alert, sound, badge). In the foreground no banner is shown (`willPresent` returns no options, `Notifications.swift:158-163`). Tapping opens the conversation once its Hub has loaded (`Chats.swift:1225-1232`). Reading a conversation removes its delivered notifications. **There is no inline reply**: no notification category, action or text-input action exists anywhere in the mobile sources.

## 4. Web translation

**Breakpoints** (proposed; Noodle itself has one phone layout for every width):

| Width | Layout |
|---|---|
| < 768 px | Phone: stack navigation. Route `/` is the list, `/c/:hub/:thread` the conversation, pushed with a horizontal slide; back gesture or button pops. Editors as full-height bottom sheets; small choices as content-height sheets; live views and the lift overlay as full-viewport layers |
| 768-1023 px | Two panes when landscape-ish: list 320 px plus conversation; or keep the phone stack in portrait tablets. Do not copy Noodle's iPad behaviour of stretching the phone layout |
| ≥ 1024 px | Sidebar (320-360 px) plus conversation, Mac-style header; dialogs centred instead of bottom sheets |

**Conversation column.** Content padding 12 px; bubble `max-width: min(100% - 48px, 560px)` (Noodle has no cap; add one for tablets and desktop). Row gap 6 px plus 10 px reserved top padding on every row for the 16 px reaction overhang. Bubble radius 18 px, padding 12 × 8 px, 17 px body text on phones (at least 16 px in any `<input>`/`<textarea>` so iOS Safari does not zoom on focus; web platform note).

**Composer and the on-screen keyboard.** Position the composer absolutely at the bottom over the transcript; give the transcript a bottom padding of one composer line only (48 + 8 + lift); let a multi-line field grow upward to 6 lines and then scroll, without changing the transcript's padding. Bottom offset with the keyboard closed: `max(8px, env(safe-area-inset-bottom) - 6px)` (needs `viewport-fit=cover`); with it open: 8 px above the keyboard. On iOS Safari read the keyboard height from `visualViewport` (`innerHeight - visualViewport.height - visualViewport.offsetTop`) on `resize`/`scroll`; on Chromium `navigator.virtualKeyboard.overlaysContent = true` with `env(keyboard-inset-height)` or `interactive-widget=resizes-content` in the viewport meta are alternatives (web platform notes, verify per browser). Keep the composer 48 px tall with a 48 px circular plus on its left, mic or send inside the pill on the right; swap mic for send as soon as there is text or a pending file. Close the keyboard when the attach panel opens (`blur()` the field) and when the transcript is scrolled by touch.

**Scrolling.** Implement Noodle's three rules: stick to the bottom only while the bottom is visible (2 px tolerance measured above the composer); always scroll to your own sent message; for an incoming message scroll to its top only if the person was at the bottom. Use `overflow-anchor` or ID-keyed restoration when prepending earlier pages; reserve image boxes from known pixel sizes (fit 240 × 320, compact 144 × 192, minimum 44) so loads never shift.

**Touch targets and gestures.** Keep 44 px minimums: plus 48, field 48, mic and send 30 × 48 (widen to 44 on web), attach rows ≥ 48, reaction cells 44, menu items ≈ 50. Noodle's 28 px reaction badges, 22 px mention chips and 30 × 36 recording buttons are below 44 px; enlarge hit areas with padding or pseudo-elements. Long press 350 ms opens the lift overlay (press scale 0.96 during the hold; suppress `-webkit-touch-callout` and text selection during the press); provide the same actions on right-click, keyboard (`ContextMenu` key / Shift+F10) and a visible button, and the same Pin action as a swipe on touch and in the context menu (the swipe must never be the only path).

**Component mapping.**

| Noodle construct | Web (React + Tailwind + shadcn/Radix) |
|---|---|
| `NavigationStack` push | Router routes with a slide transition on phones; panes on wide screens |
| `.sheet` with detents (292, 244, medium/large) | shadcn Drawer (Vaul) with snap points, drag handle visible |
| Full-screen cover (lift overlay, live views) | Full-viewport layer with its own enter/exit animation; `inert` on the page behind |
| `Menu` with `primaryAction` on pinned circles | Click navigates; long-press or right-click opens a Radix `DropdownMenu`/`ContextMenu` |
| `.swipeActions(edge: .leading)` | Pointer-event swipe revealing an orange "Pin" action, plus the same item in the context menu |
| `.searchable` | A search `Input` pinned at the top of the list (a bottom field mimicking iOS 26 is optional) |
| Liquid Glass (`glassEffect`) | Translucent fill + `backdrop-filter: blur() saturate()` + hairline highlight; Noodle defines no numbers (system material) |
| `GlassEffectContainer` morph of plus into panel | Shared-layout animation (for example a FLIP or View Transitions) from the 48 px circle to the 230 px radius-28 panel, spring roughly 400 ms with slight overshoot |
| `UIPasteControl` | A "Paste" button calling `navigator.clipboard.readText()` on the user's tap |
| QR scanner | `BarcodeDetector` where available, otherwise a JS decoder over `getUserMedia` |
| `SFSafariViewController` preview | Most sites refuse framing; use the link card and open in a new tab |
| Quick Look gallery | Lightbox swiping through the message's files |
| `AVPlayerLooper` wallpaper | `<video muted loop playsinline autoplay poster>`; `prefers-reduced-motion` shows the poster only |

| Hidden WebRTC web view | WebRTC directly in the page |

**Safe areas.** `viewport-fit=cover`; nav bar top padding `env(safe-area-inset-top)`; the wallpaper must extend under both bars; landscape game controls and floating Done buttons must respect left/right insets (Noodle keeps the gamepad 16 px inside the safe area).

**Colours.** System blue accent and semantic surfaces map to tokens: accent (iOS system blue, light #007AFF / dark #0A84FF), `secondarySystemBackground` (light #F2F2F7 / dark #1C1C1E), `tertiarySystemBackground` (light #FFFFFF / dark #2C2C2E), `systemBackground` (#FFFFFF / #000000); these hex values are Apple's published system colours (iOS 26 may adjust them slightly), not values from the repository.

**Time.** Format list times as "2 hr ago", "1 day ago", "3 days ago"; `Intl.RelativeTimeFormat` with `style: 'short'` produces "2 hr. ago" in en-US, so a small formatter is needed to match.

## 5. Patterns worth copying, and pitfalls to avoid

### Patterns worth copying

- **Composer height equals the search field height** (48), and the composer bottom sits where the list's search field sits, so moving from list to chat feels continuous.
- **Overlay composer growth**: only one line is reserved; long drafts cover the transcript instead of jumping it.
- **Reserved reaction clearance** on every row so reacting never reflows the conversation.
- **Reserved media boxes** from server-provided pixel sizes.
- **Three-rule auto-scroll** (stick only at bottom, own message always, incoming from its top).
- **Optimistic sending with bounded, idempotent retries** (0, 2, 5, 15 s, same ID, only while newest), and a failure mark offering Try Again / Edit and Send / Delete instead of a global error.
- **Per-conversation drafts** that discard whitespace-only text.
- **Pinned circles with a one-line note** (unread reply in semibold, else the bot's status): a strong at-a-glance team view.
- **Sequential modals**: open the next sheet only after the previous one dismissed.
- **Group run grouping**: name above the first message of a run, avatar beside the last, a reserved avatar column so bubbles align.
- **Folding** at 1,200 characters or 12 lines to 8 lines with "Read more".
- **Tables as cards on small screens** that open a sortable, copyable sheet.
- **Messages-style lift overlay** with geometry that keeps the bar and menu on screen and shows the start of very tall messages.
- **Disabled composer that explains itself** through its placeholder.
- **Help Me Connect**: per-route reachability plus plain-language advice and a single Try Again.
- **Notifications that keep content off the push service** and collapse per conversation, cleared when read anywhere.
- **Wordmark pull-to-refresh** as a brand moment that also communicates pull progress.

### Pitfalls to avoid

- **No tablet layout.** iPad shows the stretched phone list and full-width bubbles; build two panes at wider breakpoints.
- **Opaque mobile bubbles vs translucent Mac bubbles** over the same wallpaper: decide one rule per theme rather than diverging silently.
- **Small touch targets** (28 px badges, 22 px mention chips, 30 × 36 recording controls).
- **Markdown leaking into previews** ("The \*\*8\*\* carries…" in the list): strip inline Markdown for list rows, notes and notifications.
- **No timestamps in the transcript** at all; consider timestamps on demand (tap or hover) and day separators.
- **Hierarchical colour surprises**: catalogue names render in tint blue only because of how SwiftUI resolves `.primary` inside list buttons; choose such colours deliberately on the web.
- **Group notifications** probably fall back to "New reply"; and there is no inline reply, which a web build could add where `Notification` actions allow it.
- **Gesture-only actions**: swipe-to-pin and long-press must have visible or keyboard equivalents.
- **In-app web preview** does not translate to the web (frame blocking); do not promise it.
